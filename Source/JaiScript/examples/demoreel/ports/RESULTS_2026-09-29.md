Using those medians and bands, natural JaiScript takes 1.68 times Lua's elapsed time in the normal layout and 1.59 times in the padded layout. It uses 27.85 to 28.93 percent less time than Squirrel. Its natural/shaped ratio is 1.020 normal and 1.011 padded.

Six P cores give natural JaiScript 4.93 to 5.00 times its own serial throughput. Lua and Squirrel use one P core in this comparison. The parallel rows therefore have a different CPU allocation.

Baseline-to-candidate changes are inside the recorded bands. No speedup or regression is credited to the correctness fixes from these samples.

## Method and limits

- Hardware: Intel Core Ultra 9 285HX, Windows, 24 logical processors. Serial affinity: CPU 0. Six-worker affinity: 0, 1, 10, 11, 12, 13, the first six CPUs in the fastest efficiency class.
- Compiler: MSVC 19.51.36252, Release, /O2 /Ob2 /Oi /Ot /GL, /LTCG, static CRT. Debugger support is compiled in and inactive; profiling is off. Lua 5.4.8 uses its stock int64/double representation; Squirrel 3.2 is built with SQUSEDOUBLE and 64-bit integers.
- Every process warms the full five-frame workload, reboots/jumps the scene and measures the same five frames. The interval includes update, render, presentation, frame construction and stream hashing.
- Round order: all configurations in table order, normal then padded, repeated twice. Three rounds give six accepted samples per runtime/layout. One workload process runs at a time.
- Each runtime's padding executable is relinked with 40 retained volatile statements in its cold help function. No scene algorithm or runtime implementation differs between the normal and padded layouts.
- This is a desktop-load measurement authorized with a 1.50-core background ceiling. Accepted OSSUARY runs used 0.123 to 1.410 background core equivalents, median 0.528.
- 120 required timings were accepted. One attempt at 1.536 background cores was rejected and rerun. Its raw record remains on file.
- Lua's layout effect is about four percent. The natural six-worker baseline contains a 65.839 ms sample and a -17.47 percent paired padding extreme. Small cross-language or worker deltas inside those spreads are not reliable optimization evidence.
- The prior 30-frame snapshot has a different stream hash and time interval. Its elapsed times must not be combined with these five-frame results.
