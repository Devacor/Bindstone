## Method and limits

- Hardware: Intel Core Ultra 9 285HX, Windows, 24 logical processors. Serial affinity: CPU 0. Six-worker affinity: 0, 1, 10, 11, 12, 13, the first six CPUs in the fastest efficiency class.
- Compiler: MSVC 19.51, Release, Ninja, /O2 /Ob2 /Oi /Ot /GL, /LTCG /OPT:REF /OPT:ICF, static CRT. Debugger support is compiled in and inactive; profiling is off. Lua 5.4.8 uses its stock int64/double representation; Squirrel 3.2 is built with SQUSEDOUBLE and 64-bit integers. Lua, sol2 and Squirrel sources are the 2026-09-29 pinned set (220 identities, all verified). Lua and Squirrel are timed from the baseline build.
- Every process warms the full five-frame workload, reboots/jumps the scene and measures the same five frames. The interval includes update, render, presentation, frame construction and stream hashing.
- Round order: all configurations in table order, normal then padded, repeated twice. Three rounds give six accepted samples per runtime/layout. One workload process runs at a time.
- Each runtime's padding executable is relinked with 40 retained volatile statements in its cold help function. No scene algorithm or runtime implementation differs between the normal and padded layouts.
- This is a desktop-load measurement with a user-authorized 1.50-core background ceiling, the same ceiling as 2026-09-29. Accepted OSSUARY runs used 0.345 to 1.495 background core equivalents, median 0.819.
- 120 required timings were accepted. Fourteen attempts at 1.526 to 2.175 background cores were rejected and rerun; no competing build, test or benchmark process was observed during any timed attempt. Their raw records remain on file.
- Lua's layout effect is about four percent. The six-worker rows carry single outliers (baseline natural 65.585 ms normal, candidate natural 56.481 ms padded, candidate shaped 52.549 ms padded) and paired padding extremes of -17.63 and +16.84 percent. Small cross-language or worker deltas inside those spreads are not reliable optimization evidence.
- The 30-frame snapshot of 2026-09-26 has a different stream hash and time interval. Its elapsed times must not be combined with these five-frame results.
