GATOR QUEST
===========

You are a gator. Six tyrants rule the six realms beyond the Moongates, each hoarding a Relic Scale:
Big Mama Snapper (the Bayou), the Drowned King (the Sunken City of Sogg), Count Crocula (Gloomfen),
the Glacier Queen (Frostfang Caverns), Emberjaw the Wyrm (Mt. Scorchmaw) and the Thunder Roc
(Cloudreach). Bring all six scales to the Shrine of the Six, north-west of Swampton, and become the
Gator Avatar. Fight, fish, mine, chop, smith, cook and brew along the way. Raccoons will steal your gold.

START
  play.bat              single player (your world is saved in the saves folder)
  host.bat              play and let friends join (port 28777; host.bat port 30000 for another)
  join.bat <address>    join a friend's game (their LAN address, e.g. join.bat 192.168.1.20)
  serve.bat             a dedicated host with no screen, for a group's persistent swamp

Use Windows Terminal (or any terminal with 24-bit color) and make the window big: at least 100x30
characters, 190 columns or more for the large tiles. Z cycles the tile size. Everything, including the
sound effects and the music, is generated by the game itself; nothing needs installing.

CONTROLS
  arrows / WASD      walk; bump a monster to fight it, bump a townsperson to talk
  SPACE or E         use what you face: talk, fish (water), mine (rocks), chop (trees), open chests
  F or TAB           pick the next target (you keep swinging while it is in reach)
  G                  pick up loot at or next to your feet
  1-9                cast a spell from your spellbook      Q  drink your best healing potion
  H                  apply a bandage                        X  hide
  I                  backpack (ENTER uses or equips, D drops)
  P                  paperdoll (ENTER takes an item off)
  K  J  B  C         skills, journal (quests), spellbook, crafting (stand near a forge, anvil or fire)
  M                  world map                              ?  help
  ENTER              chat. Commands: /pvp /who /invite name /accept /leave /p party-message /home
  ESC                clear the target, then the menu: save, load, music, sound, zoom, quit

TIPS
  Shopkeepers offer Buy and Sell. The healer and the Oracle resurrect ghosts for free.
  When you die you become a ghost: walk to a healer or a shrine to live again (your gold stays
  where you fell, waiting for you).
  Skills rise as you use them. Reagents grow wild: pick them up for your spells.
  Talk to the Mayor, the Captain, the shopkeepers and the Oracle for quests.
  Stand in tall grass or reeds and press X to hide from monsters.
  Moongates around Swampton lead to the six realms; the grate in the plaza leads to the sewers.

SAVES
  The game saves every five minutes, from the menu and when you quit. Files are plain text in the
  saves folder (or %LOCALAPPDATA%\GatorQuest\saves if this folder is read-only): world.gqw and one
  file per gator in saves\chars. A .bak copy of the previous save is kept; a damaged save loads from it.

Made entirely in JaiScript, on the stock jaiscript runner.
