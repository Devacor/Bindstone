# Whole-program comparison snapshot: 2026-09-08

JaiScript runtime source: `e6f84abaeb5ebb0ee7815bbfc615f97dda2bcdf9`.
Intel Core Ultra 9 285HX, 24 physical/logical processors, Windows x64.
Serial affinity is performance CPU 0; eight-worker affinity is CPUs
0, 1, 10, 11, 12, 13, 22 and 23. The region worker count includes the caller.
Default stack-cookie protection is enabled; this is not the /GS- experiment.

Each median has six accepted processes per layout from three A B A B rounds.
A is the normal executable; B adds 40 retained volatile statements to a cold
help function that the benchmark never executes. Each padding band is the
minimum/maximum of 100 * (B/A - 1) across neighboring accepted pairs. The band
applies to both medians on its row and includes placement and run variation;
it is not a confidence interval.

GLOOM uses 600 warm-up ticks followed by a reset and 600 measured ticks in the
same runtime, seed 666, 100x40 quad output. Script execution, frame-string
production and host frame hashing are timed. Loading, boot and warm-up are not.
JaiDOOM first replays the complete 3984-tic demo, then repeats main() in the same
engine. Its ms/tic is the measured replay's reported integer elapsed milliseconds
divided by 3984, at 120x78. Its timer excludes level/demo setup.

Serial JaiScript takes 4.14 times Lua 5.4.8 time on the normal layout and
4.09 times on the padded layout, with the per-runtime padding bands above.

Lua 5.4.8 and Lua 5.5.1 have overlapping observed ranges. LuaJIT JIT has a much
wider padding band; small deltas involving that configuration are not resolved
by this snapshot. LuaJIT JIT remains a runtime-code-generation ceiling, outside
Bindstone's iOS shipping target.

## Screening and checkpoint recovery

One benchmark process ran at a time. The background CPU ceiling stayed at
1.0 core-equivalent across the 24-core desktop. Detected compiler, test, fuzz,
client and benchmark processes block launch and invalidate overlapping samples.
Checks run every 250 ms and at exit. Other applications are covered by the
CPU screen and the coordinated timing window.

Exactly 72 required timings were accepted; 27 busy attempts were excluded:
three Lua 5.4, three Lua 5.5 and 21 LuaJIT JIT. Short LuaJIT JIT processes show
coarse CPU-accounting diagnostics, so many attempts were screened out. This is
a screened desktop result, not a zero-background measurement.

The initial controller exhausted its retry budget after 66 accepted timings.
It resumed from that exact accepted ABAB prefix after validation of binaries,
workload inputs, topology, raw logs, screening verdicts and order. The retry
budget increased to 60; the acceptance ceiling and workloads did not change.
Both controller versions, every rejected attempt and pre-resume checkpoints
are preserved. The independent audit recomputed the completed medians and bands.

## Evidence and interpretation

Raw root: `Source/JaiScript/out/astra/landing-20260908/`.

- `comparison/runs.json` and per-process logs: all 22 gates, 72 accepted
  timings and 27 rejected attempts.
- `comparison/summary.json` and `audit.json`: medians, bands, completion
  checks and recomputed ratios.
- `comparison/metadata.json`, `runner.py` and `resume-01-*`:
  input identities, CPU topology, exact controller versions and recovery history.
- `source-inputs.json` and `tree/`: fixed source archive and workload
  identities. Host-only lifetime overlays belong to the separately tested client.
- `Release-configure.log`, `Release-build.log` and `release/`:
  compiled targets, flags, binaries and PDBs.
- `tests-interpreter.log`, `tests-vm.log`, `gloom-debug-300.log`:
  full Debug and hash gates.

Verified by running: all hashes/exits, completed interleaving, unchanged inputs,
full Debug suites and the control-to-normal bands. The ratio is arithmetic from
those medians. These results do not establish performance on another machine,
a universal language-speed ratio, or a small-delta ranking for the noisy JIT
configuration. No profiling instrumentation was active during timing.
