## Method and versions

- Intel Core Ultra 9 285HX, 24 physical/logical cores.
- Serial and JaiDOOM use P core 0. Eight-worker GLOOM uses P cores
  0, 1, 10, 11, 12, 13, 22 and 23.
- One workload process at a time. The other agent's builds and tests are paused
  during timing. The fixed background screen is at most 1.0 CPU core-equivalent.
- Three interleaved ABAB rounds yield six accepted samples per configuration
  and layout. GLOOM runs 600 warm-up ticks and 600 measured ticks, seed 666,
  100x40 quad output. JaiDOOM warms its 3984-tic, 120x78 replay in the same
  engine before the measured replay. Script metrics exclude load/bootstrap.
- Every executable has a separately relinked 40-line cold-code padding control.
  The padding band is the observed minimum/maximum paired control-to-normal
  percentage change. Paired revision ranges compare candidate with baseline.
  Neither range is a confidence interval.
- The paired window accepted 72 timings, with 20 hash gates and 8 busy timing
  exclusions. The Lua window accepted 60 timings, with 20 hash gates and 23 busy
  timing exclusions. Excluded attempts remain in the raw records.
